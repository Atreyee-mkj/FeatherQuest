# FeatherQuest — User Guide

Welcome to FeatherQuest, your personal offline bird journal. This guide
walks you through everything the app can do.

---

## Getting Started

Open the app in your browser. On your phone, tap the browser's **Share →
Add to Home Screen** to install it like a native app. No account, no
sign-in — your data stays on your device.

---

## The Bottom Navigation

| Icon | Screen | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🏠 | **Home** | Timeline of all your sightings |
| 🔍 | **Search** | Find sightings by name, note, or date |
| ➕ | **New** | Log a new bird sighting |
| 🏆 | **Achievements** | Track badges and streaks |
| 👤 | **Profile** | Stats, dark mode, backup |

---

## Logging a Sighting

1. Tap ➕ in the bottom nav.
2. Fill in the bird's name (required), date, and time.
3. Optionally add a location, notes, and a category.
4. Tap **Add photo** to capture or import photos.
5. Tap **Record** to capture a bird call. Tap again to stop. Play back
   with ▶, delete with 🗑.
6. Tap **Save**.

Your sighting appears at the top of the Home timeline instantly.

---

## Editing & Deleting

Tap any card on the Home timeline to open its detail page. From there
you can:

- Edit any field, add more media, or change the category
- Toggle ⭐ favorite
- Delete the sighting (this also removes its photos and audio)

---

## Search & Filters

On the Search screen:

- Type in the search bar to match bird names, notes, or ISO dates.
- Filter chips: **Photos**, **Audio**, **Favorites**, and per-category
  filters.
- Filters combine with the search query.

---

## Categories

Manage categories from **Profile → Categories**:

- **Create** a new category with an emoji icon and color
- **Rename** an existing category
- **Delete** a category (sightings keep their data; the category label
  is cleared)

---

## Achievements

Six badges unlock automatically as you use the app:

| Badge | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 🌱 First Feather | Log your first bird |
| 🐦 Bird Explorer | 10 unique species |
| 🦅 Hawk Eye | 50 unique species |
| 📷 Photographer | 25 photos captured |
| 🎙 Audio Pioneer | 10 recordings |
| 🔥 Weekly Watcher | 7-day logging streak |

Unlock dates are stored so you can revisit your milestones.

---

## Backup & Restore

**Profile → Export backup** downloads a ZIP archive containing:

- `manifest.json` — all sightings, categories, badges, and media
  metadata
- `photos/` — every photo you've attached
- `audio/` — every recording

Keep this file safe (cloud drive, USB stick, email to yourself). It is
your full journal.

---

## Dark Mode

Toggle from **Profile → Appearance → Dark mode**. Your preference is
remembered across sessions and applied before the page renders (no
flash of light).

---

## Offline Use

After the first visit, the app runs entirely offline. Every action —
logging, playback, search, export — works without a network connection.

---

## Tips

- **Fast logging:** open ➕, type the name, tap Save. You can add media
  and notes later by tapping the card.
- **Long field trips:** the app never syncs to a server, so battery
  and data usage stay low.
- **Backups are precious:** export weekly, especially before clearing
  browser data or switching devices.

Happy birding! 🪶
